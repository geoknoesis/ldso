LDSO-Operations: Operational Rule Layer

Abstract

LDSO-Operations is a domain-agnostic RDF/OWL vocabulary for expressing the operational machinery of legal rules. It occupies Layer 5 of the Legal Document Structure Ontology (LDSO) stack, bridging the abstract deontic semantics of LDSO-Semantics (Layer 4) with domain-specific vertical ontologies (Layer 5b).

The vocabulary defines eight core concept families: OperativeRule, Trigger (five subtypes), Criterion (five subtypes), Procedure and Step, Schedule (three subtypes), Measurement, Comparator (six named individuals), and DocumentaryRequirement. Together these allow any legal obligation, permission, or prohibition to be expressed as a machine-executable rule binding activation conditions, qualifying criteria, ordered procedural steps, temporal constraints, and evidentiary requirements — all without importing any domain-specific vocabulary.

LDSO-Operations imports LDSO-Semantics, which transitively imports LDSO-Core and LDSO-Entities. All four foundational modules are required for a conformant deployment.

Introduction

Scope and Purpose

Legal texts state obligations and permissions in abstract, natural-language terms. LDSO-Semantics (Layer 4) captures those deontic statements as structured RDF. But neither the text nor its deontic annotation answers the operational questions: when does the obligation activate? What must be checked before it applies? How must it be fulfilled, step by step? By when? With what evidence?

LDSO-Operations answers these questions through a single central concept — the ops:OperativeRule — that binds a trigger, criteria, a procedure, a schedule, and documentary requirements into a machine-executable rule. The rule points back to its legal grounding via ops:operativizes.

The module is deliberately domain-agnostic: it contains no trade, environmental, financial, or other domain vocabulary. Domain verticals (Layer 5b) subclass LDSO-Operations concepts and add domain-specific constraints.

Design Principles

  1. Domain-agnostic infrastructure. LDSO-Operations contains no domain vocabulary. Any domain term (HS code, emission category, financial ratio) belongs in a Layer 5b vertical that imports this module and subclasses its concepts.
  2. Trigger–Criterion–Procedure pattern. Every operative rule is composed of exactly three orthogonal concerns: what fires it (trigger), what qualifies it (criteria), and how it is executed (procedure). These are never conflated into a single node.
  3. Cross-layer grounding via ops:operativizes. Every OperativeRule SHOULD link to the Layer 4 NormativeStatement it implements. This preserves the audit trail from operational rule back to legal text.
  4. Conjunctive criteria, disjunctive triggers. Multiple ops:hasCriterion assertions are interpreted conjunctively (all must be met). Multiple ops:hasTrigger assertions are interpreted disjunctively (any one fires the rule).
  5. Named individuals for enumerated values. Comparator operators are modelled as named individuals of ops:Comparator rather than string literals, enabling OWL reasoning over comparison logic.
  6. Stepwise procedures with linked-list traversal. Procedures aggregate Steps ordered by ops:stepOrder. The optional ops:nextStep chain enables graph traversal without sorting.
  7. Quality parity with lower layers. Every class has disjointness axioms, rdfs:isDefinedBy, and a SHACL shape. Every property declares domain, range, and applicable OWL characteristics (owl:FunctionalProperty, owl:AsymmetricProperty, owl:IrreflexiveProperty).

Trigger Hierarchy

ops:Trigger is the abstract superclass for activation conditions. Instantiate one of its five subclasses:

ClassActivated when…Typical examples
ops:EventTrigger A named real-world event occurs Entry into force, customs inspection, reportable incident
ops:ThresholdTrigger A measured value crosses a numeric boundary Import volume exceeds quota, concentration exceeds limit
ops:StatusTrigger A subject entity transitions into a specified status Goods declared temporarily admitted, entity classified non-compliant
ops:TemporalTrigger A date, duration, or period boundary is reached Annual reporting date, 30 days after notification
ops:RequestTrigger A party submits a request, application, or declaration ATA carnet presented, permit application filed, appeal submitted

ThresholdTrigger MUST carry exactly one ops:hasMeasurement. All five Trigger subclasses are mutually disjoint. 

ex:Trigger_CarnetPresented
  a ops:RequestTrigger ;
  rdfs:label "ATA carnet presented at customs post"@en ;
  ops:triggerDescription """Activated when a carnet holder presents goods accompanied
by a valid ATA carnet at a border crossing point of a Contracting Party."""@en .

Criterion Hierarchy

ops:Criterion is the abstract superclass for qualifying conditions. Multiple criteria on the same rule are conjunctive. Use one of five subclasses:

ClassTests whether…Required sub-properties
ops:EligibilityCriterion Subject possesses a required status or attribute —
ops:ClassificationCriterion Subject falls within a specified category —
ops:ThresholdCriterion A value stands in relation to a numeric threshold ops:hasMeasurement (1)
ops:DocumentaryCriterion Required documentary evidence is presented ops:hasDocumentaryRequirement (1..*)
ops:TemporalCriterion Rule is invoked within an authorized time window — 
ex:Crit_ValidCarnet
  a ops:DocumentaryCriterion ;
  rdfs:label "Valid ATA carnet criterion"@en ;
  ops:criterionDescription """The carnet holder must present a valid, unexpired ATA carnet
issued by an authorized issuing association of a Contracting Party."""@en ;
  ops:hasDocumentaryRequirement ex:DocReq_ATACarnet .

Procedure and Step

ops:Procedure aggregates an ordered set of ops:Step instances that together fulfil an operative rule. Steps are ordered by ops:stepOrder (1-based integer, unique per procedure) and optionally linked by ops:nextStep for graph traversal.

PropertyRequired onTypeConstraint
ops:hasStepProcedureops:Step IRIminCount 1
ops:stepOrderStepxsd:integer ≥ 1exactly 1; unique per procedure
ops:stepDescriptionSteprdf:langStringminCount 1
ops:performedByStep / Ruleent:Role IRIoptional
ops:nextStepStepops:Step IRIoptional; Irreflexive, Asymmetric
 
ex:Proc_CarnetAdmission
  a ops:Procedure ;
  rdfs:label "ATA carnet temporary admission procedure"@en ;
  ops:hasStep ex:Step1_VerifyCarnet ;
  ops:hasStep ex:Step2_InspectGoods ;
  ops:hasStep ex:Step3_RecordEntry ;
  ops:hasStep ex:Step4_StampCounterfoil .

ex:Step1_VerifyCarnet
  a ops:Step ;
  rdfs:label "Step 1 — Verify carnet validity"@en ;
  ops:stepOrder 1 ;
  ops:stepDescription "Check that the ATA carnet has not expired."@en ;
  ops:performedBy ex:CustomsOfficer ;
  ops:nextStep ex:Step2_InspectGoods .

Schedule Hierarchy

ops:Schedule is the abstract superclass for temporal constraints. The three subclasses are mutually disjoint:

ClassSemanticsRequired properties
ops:Deadline Fixed or relative point by which action must be taken ops:deadlineDate and/or ops:deadlineDuration (at least one)
ops:RecurringSchedule Action repeats at a fixed interval ops:recurrencePeriod (exactly 1, xsd:duration)
ops:PeriodSchedule Rule applies within a bounded time window ops:periodStart and ops:periodEnd (each exactly 1) 
ex:Sched_AdmissionDeadline
  a ops:Deadline ;
  rdfs:label "6-month temporary admission deadline"@en ;
  ops:deadlineDuration "P6M"^^xsd:duration .

ex:Sched_AnnualReport
  a ops:RecurringSchedule ;
  rdfs:label "Annual reporting schedule"@en ;
  ops:recurrencePeriod "P1Y"^^xsd:duration .

Measurement and Comparator

ops:Measurement pairs a numeric value, a unit code, and a comparison operator. It is used on ops:ThresholdTrigger and ops:ThresholdCriterion instances.

The comparator is expressed by linking to one of six ops:Comparator named individuals:

IndividualSymbolMeaning
ops:Equal=Exactly equal to threshold
ops:NotEqual≠Not equal to threshold
ops:LessThan<Strictly below threshold
ops:LessThanOrEqual≤At or below threshold
ops:GreaterThan>Strictly above threshold
ops:GreaterThanOrEqual≥At or above threshold
 
ex:Meas_MinRVC
  a ops:Measurement ;
  rdfs:label "Minimum regional value content — 60%"@en ;
  ops:numericValue 60 ;
  ops:unit "%" ;
  ops:hasComparator ops:GreaterThanOrEqual .

Use standard unit codes: ISO 4217 for currencies (USD, EUR), SI symbols (kg, km), or percentage (%). Domain verticals MAY constrain the unit vocabulary via SHACL sh:pattern.

DocumentaryRequirement

ops:DocumentaryRequirement specifies documentary evidence required to satisfy a criterion or activate a rule. It MUST carry ops:documentType (rdf:langString). It MAY carry ops:issuedBy (text label) and ops:validityPeriod (xsd:duration). 

ex:DocReq_ATACarnet
  a ops:DocumentaryRequirement ;
  rdfs:label "Valid ATA Carnet"@en ;
  ops:documentType "ATA Carnet"@en ;
  ops:issuedBy "Authorized Issuing Association"@en ;
  ops:validityPeriod "P1Y"^^xsd:duration .

Usage Example — ATA Convention Temporary Admission

The following example operationalizes the temporary admission obligation from Article 2.5 of the ATA Convention, extending the LDSO-Semantics Layer 4 example with full Layer 5 operational detail.

Step 1 — Documentary Requirement

ex:DocReq_ATACarnet
  a ops:DocumentaryRequirement ;
  rdfs:label "Valid ATA Carnet"@en ;
  ops:documentType "ATA Carnet"@en ;
  ops:issuedBy "Authorized Issuing Association"@en ;
  ops:validityPeriod "P1Y"^^xsd:duration .

Step 2 — Trigger

ex:Trigger_CarnetPresented
  a ops:RequestTrigger ;
  rdfs:label "ATA carnet presented at customs post"@en ;
  ops:triggerDescription """Activated when a carnet holder presents goods accompanied
by a valid ATA carnet at a border crossing point of a Contracting Party."""@en .

Step 3 — Criteria

ex:Crit_ValidCarnet
  a ops:DocumentaryCriterion ;
  rdfs:label "Valid ATA carnet criterion"@en ;
  ops:criterionDescription "The carnet holder must present a valid, unexpired ATA carnet."@en ;
  ops:hasDocumentaryRequirement ex:DocReq_ATACarnet .

ex:Crit_GoodsMatchCarnet
  a ops:EligibilityCriterion ;
  rdfs:label "Goods match carnet description criterion"@en ;
  ops:criterionDescription """The goods must correspond in description, quantity,
and marks to the importation counterfoil."""@en .

ex:Crit_WithinAdmissionPeriod
  a ops:TemporalCriterion ;
  rdfs:label "Within authorized admission period criterion"@en ;
  ops:criterionDescription "The goods must be presented before the carnet expiry date."@en .

Step 4 — Procedure

ex:Proc_CarnetAdmission
  a ops:Procedure ;
  rdfs:label "ATA carnet temporary admission procedure"@en ;
  ops:hasStep ex:Step1_VerifyCarnet ;
  ops:hasStep ex:Step2_InspectGoods ;
  ops:hasStep ex:Step3_RecordEntry ;
  ops:hasStep ex:Step4_StampCounterfoil .

ex:Step1_VerifyCarnet
  a ops:Step ;
  rdfs:label "Step 1 — Verify carnet validity"@en ;
  ops:stepOrder 1 ;
  ops:stepDescription "Check the ATA carnet has not expired and covers the goods presented."@en ;
  ops:performedBy ex:CustomsOfficer ;
  ops:nextStep ex:Step2_InspectGoods .

ex:Step2_InspectGoods
  a ops:Step ;
  rdfs:label "Step 2 — Inspect goods against counterfoil"@en ;
  ops:stepOrder 2 ;
  ops:stepDescription "Verify goods match the importation counterfoil in description, quantity, and marks."@en ;
  ops:performedBy ex:CustomsOfficer ;
  ops:nextStep ex:Step3_RecordEntry .

ex:Step3_RecordEntry
  a ops:Step ;
  rdfs:label "Step 3 — Record entry in customs register"@en ;
  ops:stepOrder 3 ;
  ops:stepDescription "Record the temporary admission, carnet number, goods, and re-exportation date."@en ;
  ops:performedBy ex:CustomsOfficer ;
  ops:nextStep ex:Step4_StampCounterfoil .

ex:Step4_StampCounterfoil
  a ops:Step ;
  rdfs:label "Step 4 — Stamp and detach importation counterfoil"@en ;
  ops:stepOrder 4 ;
  ops:stepDescription "Stamp, sign, and detach the importation counterfoil; return carnet to holder."@en ;
  ops:performedBy ex:CustomsOfficer .

Step 5 — Operative Rule (cross-layer assembly)

ex:Rule_TemporaryAdmission
  a ops:OperativeRule ;
  rdfs:label "Temporary admission rule — ATA carnet goods"@en ;
  # Cross-layer grounding (Layer 4 → Layer 5)
  ops:operativizes ex:Oblig_Para1_Admit ;
  # Activation
  ops:hasTrigger ex:Trigger_CarnetPresented ;
  # Qualifying conditions (all conjunctive)
  ops:hasCriterion ex:Crit_ValidCarnet ;
  ops:hasCriterion ex:Crit_GoodsMatchCarnet ;
  ops:hasCriterion ex:Crit_WithinAdmissionPeriod ;
  # Execution
  ops:hasProcedure ex:Proc_CarnetAdmission ;
  # Temporal constraint
  ops:hasSchedule ex:Sched_AdmissionDeadline ;
  # Evidence
  ops:hasDocumentaryRequirement ex:DocReq_ATACarnet ;
  # Responsible role
  ops:performedBy ex:CustomsOfficer .

ex:Sched_AdmissionDeadline
  a ops:Deadline ;
  rdfs:label "6-month temporary admission deadline"@en ;
  ops:deadlineDuration "P6M"^^xsd:duration .

Competency Questions

The following questions define what LDSO-Operations is designed to answer. Each is accompanied by a SPARQL query against a conformant dataset.

CQ-O-01 — All operative rules and the norms they implement

PREFIX ops: <https://w3id.org/ldso/operations#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?rule ?ruleLabel ?norm WHERE {
  ?rule a ops:OperativeRule ;
        rdfs:label ?ruleLabel ;
        ops:operativizes ?norm .
  FILTER(lang(?ruleLabel) = "en")
}

CQ-O-02 — All criteria of a given rule, ordered by type

PREFIX ops: <https://w3id.org/ldso/operations#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?criterion ?criterionLabel ?type WHERE {
  ex:Rule_TemporaryAdmission ops:hasCriterion ?criterion .
  ?criterion a ?type ;
             rdfs:label ?criterionLabel .
  FILTER(lang(?criterionLabel) = "en")
  FILTER(?type != owl:NamedIndividual)
}

CQ-O-03 — Ordered steps of a procedure

PREFIX ops: <https://w3id.org/ldso/operations#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?order ?stepLabel ?role WHERE {
  ex:Proc_CarnetAdmission ops:hasStep ?step .
  ?step ops:stepOrder ?order ;
        rdfs:label ?stepLabel .
  OPTIONAL { ?step ops:performedBy/rdfs:label ?role . FILTER(lang(?role) = "en") }
  FILTER(lang(?stepLabel) = "en")
}
ORDER BY ?order

CQ-O-04 — All documentary requirements for a given rule

PREFIX ops: <https://w3id.org/ldso/operations#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?docType ?issuedBy ?validity WHERE {
  ex:Rule_TemporaryAdmission ops:hasDocumentaryRequirement ?docReq .
  ?docReq ops:documentType ?docType .
  OPTIONAL { ?docReq ops:issuedBy ?issuedBy }
  OPTIONAL { ?docReq ops:validityPeriod ?validity }
  FILTER(lang(?docType) = "en")
}

CQ-O-05 — Rules with ThresholdCriteria and their measurement values

PREFIX ops: <https://w3id.org/ldso/operations#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?rule ?value ?unit ?comparator WHERE {
  ?rule a ops:OperativeRule ;
        ops:hasCriterion ?crit .
  ?crit a ops:ThresholdCriterion ;
        ops:hasMeasurement ?meas .
  ?meas ops:numericValue ?value ;
        ops:unit ?unit ;
        ops:hasComparator ?comparator .
}

CQ-O-06 — Which rules are in effect on a given date?

PREFIX ops:  <https://w3id.org/ldso/operations#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>
PREFIX xsd:  <http://www.w3.org/2001/XMLSchema#>

BIND("2026-06-01"^^xsd:date AS ?today)

SELECT ?rule ?ruleLabel WHERE {
  ?rule a ops:OperativeRule ;
        ops:hasSchedule ?sched .
  ?sched ops:periodStart ?start .
  OPTIONAL { ?sched ops:periodEnd ?end }
  FILTER(?start <= ?today)
  FILTER(!BOUND(?end) || ?end >= ?today)
  OPTIONAL { ?rule rdfs:label ?ruleLabel }
}
ORDER BY ?ruleLabel

CQ-O-07 — Which triggers are associated with event-based rules?

PREFIX ops:  <https://w3id.org/ldso/operations#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?rule ?ruleLabel ?trigger ?triggerLabel WHERE {
  ?rule a ops:OperativeRule ;
        ops:hasTrigger ?trigger .
  ?trigger a ops:EventTrigger .
  OPTIONAL { ?rule    rdfs:label ?ruleLabel    . FILTER(LANG(?ruleLabel)    = "en") }
  OPTIONAL { ?trigger rdfs:label ?triggerLabel . FILTER(LANG(?triggerLabel) = "en") }
}
ORDER BY ?ruleLabel

Extending via Domain Verticals

Layer 5b domain verticals import LDSO-Operations and subclass its concepts to add domain-specific constraints without duplicating the rule machinery. A trade vertical, for example, would:

  • Subclass ops:ClassificationCriterion as trade:HSClassificationCriterion and add a trade:hsCode property constrained to the HS chapter vocabulary.
  • Subclass ops:ThresholdCriterion as trade:RegionalValueContentCriterion and constrain ops:unit to "%" via SHACL.
  • Subclass ops:DocumentaryRequirement as trade:CertificateOfOriginRequirement and fix ops:documentType to "Certificate of Origin"@en.

The key invariant: all domain verticals inherit the trigger–criterion–procedure pattern and the SHACL shapes from LDSO-Operations, ensuring cross-vertical consistency at the operational layer. 

@prefix trade: <https://example.org/trade-ontology#> .
@prefix ops:   <https://w3id.org/ldso/operations#> .

trade:HSClassificationCriterion
  a owl:Class ;
  rdfs:subClassOf ops:ClassificationCriterion ;
  rdfs:label "HS Classification Criterion"@en ;
  skos:definition "A criterion requiring that goods be classified under a specified HS heading or chapter."@en .

trade:hsCode
  a owl:DatatypeProperty ;
  rdfs:domain trade:HSClassificationCriterion ;
  rdfs:range  xsd:string ;
  rdfs:label "HS code"@en .

Appendix C — Disjointness Axioms

The following disjointness axioms are asserted in the ontology:

  • All five Trigger subclasses are pairwise disjoint (owl:AllDisjointClasses).
  • All five Criterion subclasses are pairwise disjoint (owl:AllDisjointClasses).
  • All three Schedule subclasses are pairwise disjoint (owl:AllDisjointClasses).
  • ops:OperativeRule is disjoint with ops:Trigger, ops:Criterion, ops:Procedure, ops:Schedule, ops:Measurement, and ops:DocumentaryRequirement.
  • ops:Trigger is disjoint with ops:Criterion and ops:Procedure.
  • ops:Criterion is disjoint with ops:Procedure.
  • ops:Procedure is disjoint with ops:Step.
  • ops:Step is disjoint with ops:Schedule.
  • ops:Measurement is disjoint with ops:Comparator.
