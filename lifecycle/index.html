LDSO-Lifecycle is an RDF/OWL ontology module that models the temporal and amendment lifecycle of legal documents and provisions. It defines classes and properties for amendment instructions, legal effects (insertions, deletions, substitutions, repeals, renumberings, consolidations), versioned provisions, temporal validity intervals, and legal state transitions. LDSO-Lifecycle is part of the Legal Document Structure Ontology (LDSO) family and extends LDSO-Core.

Introduction

Legal documents do not remain static. Treaties are amended by protocols; statutes are modified by amending acts; regulations are revised; contracts are updated by endorsements. Every such change raises questions: What was the law on a given date? What instruction produced a given change? Which version of a provision is currently in force?

LDSO-Lifecycle answers these questions by providing a formal vocabulary for:

  • the events that mark transitions in a document's life;
  • the amendment instructions that mandate changes;
  • the legal effects that result from those instructions;
  • the immutable version chain of every provision;
  • temporal validity intervals and applicability;
  • legal state transitions and consolidation.

Scope and Out of Scope

LDSO-Lifecycle is scoped to lifecycle and amendment structure.

The following are out of scope and belong in other modules:

  • Legal obligations, permissions, prohibitions — see LDSO-Semantics.
  • Operational trade rules, HS triggers, tariff rates — see domain ontologies.
  • Compliance evaluation and execution results — see execution ontologies.

Design Principles

  1. Never overwrite historical legal state. Old provision versions MUST be retained in the graph. Deletion from the knowledge graph is prohibited; logical deprecation is used instead.
  2. Preserve temporal traceability. Every operative state of a provision MUST be recoverable for any past point in time.
  3. Preserve amendment provenance. Every legal effect SHOULD be traceable to the instruction that produced it.
  4. Separate instructions from effects. An amendment instruction (the textual mandate) and the resulting legal effect are distinct resources with distinct IRIs.
  5. Support point-in-time queries. The data model MUST permit efficient SPARQL queries that reconstruct the law as it stood at any date.
  6. Support very large corpora. The model MUST scale to thousands of instruments and hundreds of thousands of provision versions without requiring non-standard extensions.
  7. Layered modeling. Structural concerns (LDSO-Core), amendment lifecycle (this module), legal semantics (LDSO-Semantics), and domain rules are kept in separate layers and linked by reference, not merged.

Lifecycle Overview

A legal instrument has a lifecycle that can be modelled as a sequence of events, each triggering a state transition:

  1. Commencement — the document is formally promulgated, signed, or tabled.
  2. Entry into Force — the document becomes legally operative and binding.
  3. Amendment — one or more provisions are modified by an amending instrument.
  4. Suspension (optional) — the document's effect is temporarily halted.
  5. Expiration / Termination / Repeal — the document ceases to have any operative effect.
  6. Consolidation (optional) — an editorial process integrating all amendments into a unified text.

The following example shows USMCA entering into force:

:EIF_USMCA_2020
    a life:EntryIntoForce ;
    rdfs:label "USMCA Entry into Force — 2020-07-01"@en ;
    life:eventDate "2020-07-01"^^xsd:date ;
    life:affects   :USMCA_Original .

Amendment Model

Instruction vs. Effect

LDSO-Lifecycle rigorously distinguishes two things that are often conflated in legal informatics:

Amendment Instruction (life:AmendmentInstruction)
The textual or formal directive found in an amending instrument. It is a performative speech act: "Article 2.5, paragraph 3 is hereby replaced by the following…" The instruction identifies the target provision and the nature of the change, but it does not itself constitute the new legal state.
Legal Effect (life:LegalEffect)
The resulting change in legal state — a substitution, insertion, deletion, or repeal. The legal effect creates new provision versions and closes old ones. It is linked to the instruction via life:derivedFromInstruction. 
# The instruction — what the amending instrument says
:Instr_2028_Art2_5_Para3
    a life:AmendmentInstruction ;
    life:modifies         :USMCA_Art2_5_Para3_v1 ;
    life:modificationDate "2028-01-15"^^xsd:date ;
    life:changeDescription
        "Art. 2.5 para. 3 is replaced with revised tariff schedule."@en ;
    dct:source :USMCA_AmendmentProtocol_2028 .

# The effect — what legally results
:Sub_2028_Art2_5_Para3
    a life:Substitution ;
    life:derivedFromInstruction :Instr_2028_Art2_5_Para3 ;
    life:replaces    :USMCA_Art2_5_Para3_v1 ;
    life:createsVersion :USMCA_Art2_5_Para3_v2 ;
    life:eventDate   "2028-03-01"^^xsd:date .

Effect Types

ClassMeaningKey Properties
life:Insertion New content added without removing existing content life:inserts
life:Deletion Existing content removed life:deletes
life:Substitution Content replaced by new content life:replaces, life:createsVersion
life:Repeal Entire instrument or major unit abrogated life:repeals
life:Renumbering Structural renumbering without substantive change life:createsVersion

life:Consolidation is not a subclass of life:LegalEffect. It is a direct subclass of life:LifecycleEvent. Consolidation is an editorial process that records the accumulated result of prior amendments; it does not itself produce a legal change. See Section 7 for the full consolidation model.

Temporal Model

Validity Intervals

Every life:VersionedProvision MUST carry life:effectiveFrom. When the provision is superseded or deleted, it MUST also receive life:effectiveTo. An absent life:effectiveTo denotes indefinite (open-ended) validity. 

:Art_2_5_v1
    life:effectiveFrom "2020-07-01"^^xsd:date ;
    life:effectiveTo   "2028-02-28"^^xsd:date .

:Art_2_5_v2
    life:supersedes    :Art_2_5_v1 ;
    life:effectiveFrom "2028-03-01"^^xsd:date .   # open-ended

For cases requiring additional annotations on the interval itself (e.g., uncertainty flags or retroactivity markers), use the life:ValidityInterval class as a first-class resource linked via life:hasValidityInterval.

Temporal Applicability

Validity (when a provision exists in law) is distinct from applicability (when it governs transactions or situations). Use life:TemporalApplicability and life:hasTemporalApplicability to record retroactive, transitional, or prospective application windows.

Point-in-Time Queries

The following SPARQL query retrieves the version of Art. 2.5 Para. 3 that was in force on 2025-06-01:

PREFIX life: <https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#>
PREFIX dct:  <http://purl.org/dc/terms/>
PREFIX ex:   <https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle/example#>

SELECT ?version ?from ?to
WHERE {
    ?version a life:VersionedProvision ;
             dct:isPartOf ex:USMCA_Art2_5 ;
             life:effectiveFrom ?from .
    OPTIONAL { ?version life:effectiveTo ?to }
    BIND("2025-06-01"^^xsd:date AS ?queryDate)
    FILTER (?from <= ?queryDate)
    FILTER (!BOUND(?to) || ?to >= ?queryDate)
}

Future and Pending Amendments

Model amendments enacted but not yet operative as life:PendingModification. They carry a future life:effectiveFrom date. When that date is reached, record the transition via life:LegalStateTransition to life:EffectiveModification.

Versioning Model

Stable Identity

The legal entity (e.g., Article 2.5 of USMCA) has a persistent IRI that never changes. Its versioned instances carry temporal metadata. This separation supports reasoning at both the abstract level (the article as a whole) and the version level (the article at a specific point in time).

Version Chain

Versions form a doubly-linked list via life:previousVersion and life:nextVersion, which are sub-properties of life:supersededBy and life:supersedes respectively. life:supersedes is declared owl:AsymmetricProperty and owl:IrreflexiveProperty to prevent cyclic chains. 

:Art2_5_Para3_v1
    a life:VersionedProvision ;
    life:versionNumber   "1" ;
    life:effectiveFrom   "2020-07-01"^^xsd:date ;
    life:effectiveTo     "2028-02-28"^^xsd:date ;
    life:nextVersion     :Art2_5_Para3_v2 ;
    life:supersededBy    :Art2_5_Para3_v2 .

:Art2_5_Para3_v2
    a life:VersionedProvision ;
    life:versionNumber   "2" ;
    life:effectiveFrom   "2028-03-01"^^xsd:date ;
    life:previousVersion :Art2_5_Para3_v1 ;
    life:supersedes      :Art2_5_Para3_v1 .

Deprecated Provisions

Provisions that are no longer operative MUST NOT be removed from the graph. They SHOULD be typed as life:DeprecatedProvision and MUST carry a life:effectiveTo date. This ensures that point-in-time queries and audit trails remain intact.

Consolidation Model

A consolidated legal text integrates the current state of all provisions of an instrument, incorporating all amendments that have taken effect up to the consolidation date. Consolidations are editorial acts, not amending acts — they do not change the law. 

:Consolidated_USMCA_2028
    a life:Consolidation ;
    rdfs:label     "USMCA Consolidated Text (as of 2028-03-01)"@en ;
    life:consolidates  :USMCA_Original ;
    life:consolidates  :USMCA_AmendmentProtocol_2028 ;
    life:consolidationDate "2028-03-01"^^xsd:date ;
    life:eventDate     "2028-03-01"^^xsd:date .

:Consolidated_Art2_5_Para3
    a life:ConsolidatedProvision ;
    life:derivedFromConsolidation :Consolidated_USMCA_2028 ;
    life:derivedFromProvision     :USMCA_Art2_5_Para3_v1 ;
    life:derivedFromProvision     :USMCA_Art2_5_Para3_v2 ;
    life:effectiveFrom "2028-03-01"^^xsd:date ;
    life:versionNumber "consolidated-2028-03-01" ;
    life:stateLabel    "Consolidated" .

A life:Consolidation MUST life:consolidates at least two instruments: the original and at least one amendment. This is enforced by the SHACL shapes in shapes.ttl.

Legal States

The life:LegalStateScheme SKOS concept scheme provides a controlled vocabulary of named legal states. Use life:stateLabel for a human-readable string. For machine-processable state tracking, use life:LegalStateTransition with life:transitionsFrom and life:transitionsTo pointing to SKOS concepts in this scheme.

Concept IRILabelMeaning
life:StatePending Pending Enacted but not yet operative (future entry-into-force date)
life:StateEffective Effective Currently operative and legally binding
life:StateSuspended Suspended Temporarily not operative
life:StateExpired Expired Naturally reached end of validity period
life:StateRepealed Repealed Formally abrogated by amending instrument
life:StateDeprecated Deprecated Superseded or deleted; retained for historical reference
life:StateConsolidated Consolidated Present in a consolidated text integrating all amendments

    life:transitionsFrom life:StateEffective ;
    life:transitionsTo   life:StateDeprecated ;

Competency Questions

The following questions define the scope of what LDSO-Lifecycle is designed to answer. Each is accompanied by a SPARQL query against a conformant dataset.

CQ-L-01 — What is the current in-force expression of a given legal resource?

PREFIX ldso: <https://w3id.org/ldso/core#>
PREFIX life: <https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#>
PREFIX dct:  <http://purl.org/dc/terms/>

SELECT ?expression ?language ?issued WHERE {
  <https://example.org/ata> ldso:hasExpression ?expression .
  FILTER NOT EXISTS { ?expression life:validTo ?end }
  OPTIONAL { ?expression dct:language ?language }
  OPTIONAL { ?expression dct:issued   ?issued   }
}

CQ-L-02 — Which amendment instructions targeted a given article?

PREFIX life: <https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?instruction ?instructionLabel ?effectType WHERE {
  ?instruction a life:AmendmentInstruction ;
               life:targets <https://example.org/ata/art_5> ;
               life:producesEffect ?effect .
  ?effect a ?effectType .
  OPTIONAL { ?instruction rdfs:label ?instructionLabel }
}
ORDER BY ?instructionLabel

CQ-L-03 — What was the applicable version of a provision on a specific date?

PREFIX life: <https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#>

BIND("2024-01-01"^^xsd:date AS ?pointInTime)

SELECT ?expression ?validFrom ?validTo WHERE {
  ?expression life:validFrom ?validFrom .
  OPTIONAL { ?expression life:validTo ?validTo }
  FILTER(?validFrom <= ?pointInTime)
  FILTER(!BOUND(?validTo) || ?validTo > ?pointInTime)
}

CQ-L-04 — Which instruments have been repealed?

PREFIX life: <https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?resource ?repealedBy ?repealDate WHERE {
  ?event a life:Repeal ;
         life:affects ?resource .
  OPTIONAL { ?event life:eventDate ?repealDate }
  OPTIONAL { ?event life:producedBy ?repealedBy }
}
ORDER BY ?repealDate

CQ-L-05 — What is the version chain of a given legal resource?

PREFIX life: <https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#>
PREFIX dct:  <http://purl.org/dc/terms/>

SELECT ?version ?issued WHERE {
  <https://example.org/ata/v1> life:supersedes* ?version .
  OPTIONAL { ?version dct:issued ?issued }
}
ORDER BY ?issued

CQ-L-06 — Which consolidated texts incorporate a given amending act?

PREFIX life: <https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#>
PREFIX rdfs: <http://www.w3.org/2000/01/rdf-schema#>

SELECT ?consolidated ?label WHERE {
  ?consolidated life:consolidates <https://example.org/protocol_2020> .
  OPTIONAL { ?consolidated rdfs:label ?label }
}

Conformance

A conforming LDSO-Lifecycle implementation MUST:

  1. Use the namespace https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle# for all LDSO-Lifecycle terms.
  2. Type every lifecycle event node as life:LifecycleEvent or one of its subclasses: life:Entry, life:Suspension, life:Repeal, life:Expiry.
  3. Link every life:AmendmentInstruction to a target provision via life:targets and to its resulting life:AmendmentEffect via life:producesEffect.
  4. Represent document version chains using life:supersedes and life:supersededBy only between ldso:LegalExpression nodes. These properties are asymmetric and irreflexive — a document cannot supersede itself.
  5. Represent temporal applicability via life:validFrom and life:validTo on ldso:LegalExpression or ldso:LegalElement nodes. If life:validTo is absent, the resource is considered currently in force.
  6. Pass all SHACL shapes defined in shapes.ttl without violations.

A conforming implementation SHOULD:

  • Record prov:wasDerivedFrom on consolidated versions pointing to the base version and all amending instruments.
  • Assert life:consolidates to link a consolidated text to the legislative instruments incorporated into it.

Class Definitions

life:LifecycleEvent

IRI: https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#LifecycleEvent

Label: Lifecycle Event

Definition: An event that affects the legal status, validity, or content of a legal document or provision.

Subclasses: life:Modification, life:LegalEffect, life:AmendmentInstruction, life:Consolidation, life:Commencement, life:EntryIntoForce, life:Termination, life:Suspension, life:Expiration

Required properties: life:eventDate (MUST), rdfs:label (SHOULD)

life:Modification

IRI: https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#Modification

Label: Modification

SubclassOf: life:LifecycleEvent

Definition: A lifecycle event that changes the text, structure, or legal effect of a provision or document.

Subclasses: life:PendingModification, life:EffectiveModification

life:AmendmentInstruction

IRI: https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#AmendmentInstruction

Label: Amendment Instruction

SubclassOf: life:LifecycleEvent

Definition: The textual or formal direction, found within an amending legal instrument, that specifies what change is to be made to the target provision.

Required properties: life:modifies (MUST), life:modificationDate (MUST), life:changeDescription (SHOULD), dct:source (SHOULD)

Usage note: An AmendmentInstruction is the cause. Its corresponding LegalEffect is the consequence. Both MUST exist as distinct resources.

life:LegalEffect

IRI: https://w3id.org/ldso/lifecycle#LegalEffect

Label: Legal Effect

SubclassOf: life:LifecycleEvent

Definition: The actual legal consequence of an amendment instruction on the state of the legal corpus.

Subclasses: life:Insertion, life:Deletion, life:Substitution, life:Repeal, life:Renumbering, life:Consolidation

Required properties: life:eventDate (MUST), life:derivedFromInstruction (SHOULD)

life:Insertion

SubclassOf: life:LegalEffect

Definition: New content added to an existing legal instrument without removing existing content.

Required properties: life:inserts (MUST)

life:Deletion

SubclassOf: life:LegalEffect

Definition: Existing content removed from an instrument. The removed provision MUST receive life:effectiveTo.

Required properties: life:deletes (MUST)

life:Substitution

SubclassOf: life:LegalEffect

Definition: Existing content replaced by new content. Closes the old version and opens a new one.

Required properties: life:replaces (MUST), life:createsVersion (MUST)

life:Repeal

SubclassOf: life:LegalEffect

Definition: An entire instrument or major structural unit abrogated.

Required properties: life:repeals (MUST)

life:Renumbering

SubclassOf: life:LegalEffect

Definition: Structural renumbering without substantive content change.

Required properties: life:createsVersion (MUST)

life:Consolidation

SubclassOf: life:LifecycleEvent

Definition: An editorial process that assembles the current state of a legal instrument by integrating all amendments into a unified text at a given date. Consolidation records accumulated prior effects; it does not itself produce a legal change.

Required properties: life:consolidates (MUST, ≥2), life:consolidationDate (MUST)

life:VersionedProvision

SubclassOf: life:VersionedResource

Definition: A version-specific representation of a legal provision valid during a defined temporal interval.

Required properties: life:effectiveFrom (MUST), life:versionNumber (MUST), life:effectiveTo (MUST when superseded)

Usage note: Old instances MUST NEVER be deleted. New amendments MUST create new instances.

life:ConsolidatedProvision

SubclassOf: life:VersionedProvision

Definition: A provision in a consolidated text, integrating the original and all amendments to the consolidation date.

Required properties: life:derivedFromConsolidation (MUST), life:derivedFromProvision (MUST, ≥1)

life:DeprecatedProvision

SubclassOf: life:VersionedProvision

Definition: A provision that is no longer operative, retained for historical reference.

Required properties: life:effectiveTo (MUST)

life:ValidityInterval

Definition: A time interval during which a legal resource is legally operative.

Required properties: life:effectiveFrom (MUST)

life:TemporalApplicability

Definition: A specification of when a legal rule applies in time, potentially distinct from its validity interval (supports retroactive, transitional, prospective application).

life:LegalStateTransition

Definition: A transition between two named legal states triggered by a lifecycle event.

Required properties: life:transitionsFrom (MUST), life:transitionsTo (MUST), life:eventDate (MUST), life:affects (MUST)

life:DeprecatedProvisionShape life:DeprecatedProvision effectiveTo (1)

Example shape:

life:VersionedProvisionShape
    a sh:NodeShape ;
    sh:targetClass life:VersionedProvision ;
    sh:property [
        sh:path     life:effectiveFrom ;
        sh:minCount 1 ;
        sh:maxCount 1 ;
        sh:datatype xsd:date ;
        sh:message  "Every VersionedProvision MUST have exactly one life:effectiveFrom."@en ;
    ] ;
    sh:sparql [
        sh:message "life:effectiveTo MUST be >= life:effectiveFrom."@en ;
        sh:select  """
            SELECT $this WHERE {
                $this life:effectiveFrom ?from ;
                      life:effectiveTo   ?to .
                FILTER (?to < ?from)
            }
        """ ;
    ] .
